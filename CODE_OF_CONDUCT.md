# Community guidelines

Be respectful, constructive and welcoming. Focus criticism on ideas and code.
Respect people's privacy, experience and time. Harassment, discrimination,
threats, doxxing and repeated disruptive behavior are not acceptable in project
issues, pull requests or other community spaces.

Maintainers may edit/remove harmful content, close disruptive conversations or
restrict participation. These guidelines apply to maintainers as well as
contributors. For abusive content, use GitHub's **Report content** feature;
for an ordinary project concern, contact the maintainer through the relevant
issue without reposting private or harmful information.
